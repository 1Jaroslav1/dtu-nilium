# S13 — Chronic-cough research groups reachable from Copenhagen

**Summary:** Desk research on S13. Verdict: **confirmed**. At least four Danish groups publish on chronic cough (Rigshospitalet, Herlev-Gentofte, Aarhus, University of Southern Denmark). **None uses objective cough monitoring**, and Denmark has no tertiary cough clinics.

## Verdict

**confirmed:** 4 groups [DATA].

**What it changes:** we have interview targets in Copenhagen. **The finding is two-sided:** Danish cough research is registry- and questionnaire-based, with no objective monitoring and no specialist clinics. That could mean an open gap (cost/access blocks them) or no demand (they don't need counts). **Only an interview tells which.** That question becomes the top interview question.

## For interviews

- "In your last chronic-cough study, how did you measure cough? Did you consider an objective monitor, and what stopped you?"
