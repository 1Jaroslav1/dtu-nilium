# S8 — Cost of current monitoring

**Summary:** Desk research on S8. Verdict: **partly**. The gold-standard device costs about AUD 19,000 (≈ €11–12k) to buy, and its semi-automatic analysis takes about 87 min of analyst time per 24 h recording. Per-recording or academic prices aren't public.

## Hypothesis

> Objective cough monitoring costs hundreds of euros or more per patient-recording, enough to limit small academic studies.

## Pre-committed criteria

| | Criterion | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmation | Published price or labour data implying ≥ €100 per recording | "Too expensive" holds; a low-cost device has a value story |
| Refutation | Cheap options (< €20/recording) or low analysis effort widely available | No cost wedge; drop the cost angle |

## Evidence

| Claim | Value | Tier | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VitaloJAK purchase price | AUD 19,027 (≈ €11–12k) | C (retail listing) | [ESTIMATE] | Axis Health listing |
| Analysis model | Central, semi-automatic; Vitalograph's analysts deliver the counts, sold as a study service | C | vendor claim | Vitalograph cough studies page |
| Analyst time | ~87 min per 24 h recording for VitaloJAK vs ~5 min for the Leicester Cough Monitor | A | [DATA] | JTD (Shim et al.), cited comparison |
| Validation abstract | Up to 10 min analysis per recording | B | [DATA] | ResearchGate abstract P159 |
| Hyfe phone app | ~€30/year consumer | C | [DATA] (app store) | [[S7 — Research tier already exists]] |
| Per-recording / academic fee | **not public** | — | gap | — |

**Conflicts:** analyst time of 87 min vs up to 10 min for VitaloJAK, probably different protocols. Plan on the conservative reading, that analysis is a real but uncertain cost.

## Verdict

**partly**

**Why:** the clinical-grade route is clearly expensive (device ≈ €11k + analyst labour). The cost per recording that a researcher actually pays is unknown, and Hyfe may already be cheap.

**What it changes:** "cheaper than VitaloJAK" is easy to claim but isn't a moat, because Hyfe likely already wins on price. The cost story only works against Hyfe's academic price, which is still unknown (see S7 next step).
