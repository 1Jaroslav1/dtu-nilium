# S11 — Why the FDA rejected gefapixant

**Summary:** Desk research on S11. Verdict: **confirmed**. The FDA rejected gefapixant twice. 2022: questions about **efficacy measurement, mainly the cough counting system**. Dec 2023: no substantial evidence of effectiveness; reviewers called the reduction small and its clinical meaning uncertain. The advisory committee voted 12–1 against.

## Hypothesis

> The FDA declined gefapixant because the measured effect was too small or not clinically meaningful, which makes measurement quality a live issue ("why now").

## Verdict

**confirmed** (tier B reporting, consistent across outlets and Merck's own statement).

**What it changes:** the "why now" holds, and it's even sharper than assumed. **The cough counting method itself was questioned.** But the people who feel this are **pharma sponsors and CROs**, a market owned by Vitalograph, Hyfe and C-mo (see [[../07 ⚔️ Competition map]]). It supports the measurement problem, not our ability to win it.
