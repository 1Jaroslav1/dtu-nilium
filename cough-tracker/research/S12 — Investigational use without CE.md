# S12 — Can academic studies use a non-CE monitor?

**Summary:** Desk research on S12. Verdict: **confirmed, with conditions**. Under EU MDR Article 82, investigator-led studies can use a non-CE-marked device when the results aren't used for CE marking. In Denmark these go to the Medical Research Ethics Committees, and national rules apply. So no notified body is needed, but **ethics approval is**.

## Hypothesis

> EU academic studies can use a non-CE-marked cough monitor as an investigational device, so the research route needs no Class IIa certification.

## Pre-committed criteria

| | Criterion | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmation | Official MDR guidance or national authority pages describing a route for non-CE devices in non-commercial studies | The research route is legally open without a notified body |
| Refutation | Non-CE devices barred from academic studies, or a full Art. 62 authorisation always required | The research route is as heavy as a product launch, so drop it |

## Verdict

**confirmed, with conditions:** legal route exists [DATA], but it goes through ethics committees and national rules, and the sponsor carries safety and data-quality duties.

**What it changes:** a research prototype is legally feasible in Denmark without a notified body. It's still a real step (an ethics application, weeks to months), so a hackathon demo uses team members only, not patients.
