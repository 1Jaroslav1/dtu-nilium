**Summary:** Pre-committed criteria and three outcomes (green/yellow/red) for continuing, pivoting or stopping the cough tracker. Set before seeing any results.

**Gate date:** *(to fill. Proposal: 3 weeks from the first interview.)*

On the gate date, round 1 counts as delivered if we bring:

- **10 conversations** logged in [[03 📋 Interviews — tracker]], with at least 3 from each of the two leading segments.
- **S1–S4 desk checks done**: mic feasibility, competitors and prices, COPD evidence, regulatory class.
- **A one-night prototype test:** the device's count compared with a manual count from a recording made with consent, e.g. a team member with a cold. Report precision/recall honestly.

## Thresholds (pre-committed)

| Criterion | Green | Yellow | 
|---|---|---|
| **H6:** Danish cough researchers who say cost/effort (not lack of need) stopped them using objective counts | ≥2 of 3 | ≤1 of 3 |
| **H7:** of those, how many prefer bedside/no-wear over Hyfe's watch, once they hear both | ≥1 | 0 |
| Segments where ≥50% **act on a cough count today** | ≥1 | 0 |
| Of those, % with a workaround | ≥40% | <40% |
| Pilots agreed **with a date** | ≥1 | 0 |
| Prototype false counts per night (quiet room) | ≤ *(set after S1)* | above |

## Three outcomes

**Green: delivered, and the market confirmed.** One segment acts on the count, has a workaround and gave a pilot date. Decision: choose the beachhead, finish the persona and offer, and build L1 for that pilot. *(Next step + deadline to fill.)*

**Yellow: delivered, but the market didn't confirm.** Either (a) conversations revealed a different signal people act on (breathing rate, night-time getting up, falls), which means pivot the loop and keep the kit, or (b) nobody acts on a cough count. Decision within 3 days: one more round with a new written threshold, or drop the idea and return to [[../brainstorm.md]].

**Red: the minimum wasn't delivered.** Fewer than 10 conversations or no prototype test. Nothing to evaluate, so close it and discuss what blocked us.

**Green and yellow are about the market. Red is about execution.** We record them separately on purpose.
