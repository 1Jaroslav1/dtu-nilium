# Interview script — Cough Tracker (round 1)

**Summary:** Mom Test discovery script for clinicians, trial staff, care-home nurses and parents. It asks about specific past events and their cost, not opinions about our device.
**Tags:** #cough-tracker #discovery #interview-script #mom-test
**Created:** 2026-11-21
**Last Updated:** 2026-11-21

---

**Length:** 20–25 min. **Never show the device or describe the product until the closing.** The interviewee talks about 90% of the time.

## Before the call

Role, setting (clinic / trial / care home / home), which condition, how many patients they follow. This goes into the columns of [[03 📋 Interviews — tracker]].

## Block 1 — the last specific patient/night (most time) → H1, H2

> "Think of the last patient (or night) where cough was part of the picture. Walk me through what happened, step by step."

Then dig. How did you know how much they were coughing? Who told you? Did it change anything you did: a dose, a visit, a referral, a call?

**Listen for:** a decision that depended on the cough amount. If there isn't one, that's the most important data point of the call.

**Added after desk research (H1 proxy):**
- Clinic: "Tell me about the last patient whose cough you tried to quantify. What did you use, and did the number change what you did?"
- Care home: "Think of the last resident who developed a chest infection. What was the first thing anyone noticed, and at what time of day?"
- Trial / CRO (S5): "In your last cough study, how many recording days did each patient do, and what happened when a recording failed or the day was unusual?"
- Care home (S4): "When did you last bring in a new monitoring device? Who had to approve it?"

## Block 2 — when it went wrong, and what it cost → H2, H3

> "When did you last find out too late that someone's cough or breathing was getting worse? What did it cost?"

Dig: an admission? An extra visit? A trial patient dropped? **"What did you do so it wouldn't happen again?"** The workaround is the signal.

## Block 3 — what they use today → H3

> "The last time you needed to know how much someone coughs, how did you get the number?"

A diary, an app, recall, a hospital monitor? How long does it take? Who fills it in? How often is it missing?

## Block 4 — privacy and the bedroom → H4 (patients/parents only)

> "Has anyone ever recorded you or your child sleeping, for a doctor or an app? How did that go?"

Ask about past behaviour. **Don't ask "would you accept a mic?"**

## Block 5 — environment → H5 (cut first if short on time)

> "When did you last tell a patient anything about their room: heating, humidity, air?"

## Closing

- "Who else should I talk to?" Always ask.
- If pain scored 2–3: "If in a few weeks I had something that gives you that number objectively, could we try it with one patient? When?" Get **a date**. Don't ask "how much would you pay".

## Don'ts

Don't pitch. Don't say "AI-powered health platform". Don't record a hypothetical "yes" as evidence. Don't lead ("cough tracking would help you, right?").

## Mapping to hypotheses

| Block | Hypothesis | What it fills in the tracker |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | H1, H2 | "Acts on count" (Y/N + what), current source of the number |
| 2 | H2, H3 | Cost of a late catch, workaround |
| 3 | H3 | Current tool, time, gaps |
| 4 | H4 | Privacy behaviour |
| 5 | H5 | Environment advice given (Y/N) |
