## Persona v0 — end users

| Candidate | What they do | Motivation to use |
|---|---|---|
| **Patient (COPD, 60+)** | Plugs it in by the bed, presses "start night" | **Weak.** It's the nurse's need, not theirs, unless the screen gives them something useful. **Weakest link.** |
| Nurse / care coordinator | Reads the counts and calls when the trend jumps | Medium: only if it saves calls rather than adding alerts |

**Warning:** if the patient forgets to turn it on, there's no data and the whole loop fails. Design for **zero-touch routine use** (always on, auto-detect night).
