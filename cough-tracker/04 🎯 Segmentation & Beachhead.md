## Segmentation axes

| Axis | Values | Pain hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| Condition | chronic cough · COPD · asthma (child) · respiratory infection (elderly) | Strongest where the count is already an accepted metric (chronic cough) |
| Setting | clinical trial · hospital/clinic RPM · care home · home (self-pay) | Pain is concentrated where someone is paid to watch the number |
| Who pays | pharma / CRO · health system · care-home operator · patient/parent | B2B payers have budget, consumers rarely pay for health gadgets |
| Who uses | patient · parent · nurse · trial coordinator | The user who sleeps next to it has the weakest motivation |

## Beachhead candidates

| # | Candidate | Axis combination | Why a candidate | Main risk | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Chronic-cough clinical trials** | chronic cough × trial × pharma/CRO pays × patient uses | Cough frequency is already a trial endpoint, so the count *is* the decision | Requires clinical-grade validation, long sales cycle, few buyers | desk |
| B2 | **COPD remote monitoring** | COPD × clinic RPM × health system pays × patient uses | A cough increase *may* precede exacerbations, which cost hospital admissions | Unproven that the count changes treatment; clinic workflows are hard to enter | desk, to verify (S3) |
| B3 | **Parents of asthmatic children** | asthma × home × parent pays and uses | Parents worry at night and doctors ask "how much did they cough?" | Consumers rarely pay, and child-bedroom audio raises privacy concerns | assumption |
| B4 | **Care homes, night respiratory watch** | infection × care home × operator pays × nurse acts | Night staff is thin, and catching pneumonia early matters | Is cough the signal staff need, or do they need falls / breathing? | assumption |
