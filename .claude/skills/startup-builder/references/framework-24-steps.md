# The master arc — Aulet's 24 Steps (skeleton + fallback)

**This is a skeleton and a fallback.** The user keeps their own `24 steps` note in
`General Knowledge and Tools/`. Read that first — it is the source of truth and may
phrase, group, or prioritize steps differently. Use this file only to (a) understand the
overall shape when the vault is reachable, or (b) work when the vault is locked, in which
case tell the user you're on the built-in version.

Bill Aulet's *Disciplined Entrepreneurship* organizes new-product work into 24 steps
across six themes. The A→Z phases in SKILL.md map onto these themes. The point of the
framework is **order**: you cannot sensibly size a market before you know the customer,
or design an offer before you know the pain.

## The six themes and their steps

**1. Who is your customer?**
1. Market segmentation — brainstorm broadly, don't converge yet
2. Select a beachhead market — the one narrow market you win first
3. Build an end-user profile
4. Calculate the TAM for the beachhead
5. Profile the persona for the beachhead — one real, named archetype
6. Full life cycle use case — how the customer discovers, buys, uses, pays

**2. What can you do for the customer?**
7. High-level product specification (visual, not built)
8. Quantify the value proposition — before vs after, in the customer's own metric
9. Identify your next 10 customers — does the persona generalize?
10. Define your core — the one thing hard for others to copy

**3. How does the customer acquire your product?**
11. Chart the competitive position — on the two axes the customer cares about
12. Determine the customer's decision-making unit (DMU) — who champions, who pays, who vetoes
13. Map the acquisition process — the real steps to a sale

**4. How do you make money?**
14. TAM for follow-on markets
15. Design a business model
16. Set your pricing framework
17. LTV of an acquired customer
18. COCA — cost to acquire a customer (LTV must clear COCA with margin)

**5. How do you design & build your product?**
19. Identify key assumptions
20. Test key assumptions — cheaply, before building
21. Define the MVBP (Minimum Viable Business Product) — smallest thing that closes the loop AND that someone pays for
22. Show that "the dogs will eat the dog food" — real usage, not opinion

**6. How do you scale?**
23. TAM for next markets / expansion
24. Develop a product plan — the sequence beyond the MVBP

## Two disciplines Aulet insists on, worth repeating

- **Primary market research over assumption.** Steps 1–6 are done by talking to people,
  not by reasoning from your desk. The interview method is the Mom Test (see
  `discovery-playbook.md`).
- **Single beachhead.** Resist the urge to keep options open by serving several segments.
  Aulet's whole method collapses if you don't converge on one. Breadth comes *after* you
  own the beachhead (steps 23–24), never before.
