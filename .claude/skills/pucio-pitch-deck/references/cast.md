# The storybook cast (from the example book)

These are the fairy-tale people from "Pucio robi GTM". **They are the default cast for every deck.** Your startup's real roles are mapped onto them: you don't invent new characters.

Reference pictures are in `assets/reference/` (the 10 illustrations from the example). `01-cover-cast.jpg` shows Pucio, Artur and Kasia together. `04-user-vs-payer.jpg` and `07-purchase-process.jpg` show the Boss.

## Main cast (use these 3 by default)

### Pucio, the one who sees the truth
- **Look:** a toddler about 3, messy dark-brown hair, big round dark eyes, rosy cheeks, a small curious mouth. A **red long-sleeve sweater**, dark teal trousers with rolled hems, small brown shoes. (Alternative outfit from the cover: a grey-and-white striped top with dark trousers. Pick one per deck and keep it.)
- **Acts:** points, tugs a sleeve, asks one naive question, holds up the right thing (a photo, a small card), hides or guards the confetti. Often stands on a stool or sits on a box.
- **Story job:** says or shows the obvious thing the adults miss. Never lectures.

### Artur, the founder
- **Look:** a man in his mid-30s, tousled sandy-blond curly hair, **round thick black-rimmed glasses**, a light moustache and stubble, a huge eager grin. A **white T-shirt with a small print on the chest** (in your deck, put your product name there), a black-and-grey tattoo on the forearm, a black smartwatch, baggy beige chinos, white sneakers.
- **Props:** a grey laptop, a **tall polka-dot confetti tube**, an endless paper roll of flowcharts, a flipchart.
- **Story job:** plays your founder. Lovable, too eager: too many features, a 40-page offer, celebrates too early. Learns from Pucio.

### Kasia, the customer
- **Look:** a woman about 30, **big voluminous dark-brown curly hair**, a **mustard-yellow button-up shirt** with rolled sleeves, wide teal-green trousers, brown loafers. Often holds a small spiral notepad.
- **Story job:** plays your user, the person who feels the pain. Busy, kind, short on time. Her struggle is always visible: a clock, a locked laptop, a pile of papers.
- **Adapting her:** keep her look and name, and change only her situation to fit your market ("Kasia is a nurse", "Kasia's son coughs at night").

## Optional 4th

### The Boss (Kasia's boss), the one who pays
- **Look:** a man about 60, swept-back grey hair, a **navy blazer**, a light-blue shirt, grey trousers, brown shoes. Holds a **brown wallet**. Hand on chin, thoughtful, a little sceptical.
- **Use only if** the person who pays or decides is not the person who uses (B2B, parent vs doctor, insurer).

## Optional 5th (cameo, at most one, rarely)

From the "market that buys" and "who decides" pages. Use one only if the story breaks without them:
- **The Intern:** a young woman with a messy bun, a sage-green sweater, jeans, white sneakers, reading a book. "Wrong contact" type.
- **The Sceptic:** a woman with a dark bob, a pink top, black trousers, a shoulder bag, arms crossed. "Doesn't believe it works."
- **The No-budget man:** a bearded man in a green overshirt and jeans, looking into an almost empty wallet.
- **The Not-interested woman:** a woman with a ponytail, a blue shirt, rust trousers, a hand raised in polite refusal, holding a checklist.

## Mapping your startup onto the cast

| Your reality | Played by |
|---|---|
| Founder / CEO | Artur |
| User with the pain | Kasia |
| Buyer / payer, if different | The Boss |
| The insight, the "obvious truth" | Pucio |
| Wrong customer type you learned to avoid | one cameo (optional) |

**Team page:** Artur stands in for the founding team by default. If there are co-founders, draw them **in the same storybook style** next to Artur (each with one signature outfit and one prop tied to their real skill). They count toward the 5-character limit, so with co-founders, leave out the Boss and cameos on that page.

**About Artur:** in the example, Artur may be a caricature of a real person. If your audience knows the original, keep Artur's silhouette (glasses, white T-shirt, chinos) but give him your founder's hair and face, and say so in the character sheet.
